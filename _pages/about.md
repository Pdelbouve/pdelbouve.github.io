Welcome to my website ! I am a PhD candidate in economics at the Paris School of Economics, affiliated with the [Farhi Innovation Lab](https://www.farhi-innovation-lab.fr/fr/) at the Collège de France and the Laboratory for Innovation and Future Transformations ([LIFT](https://heclift.github.io/website/)) in HEC Paris. I also work as an economist at the [Direction Générale des Entreprises](https://www.entreprises.gouv.fr/), where I work on public policies to develop AI in France.

My research explores the relationship between artificial intelligence, labor and firm dynamics, studying both how AI changes competitive dynamics across firms and workers and how competition shapes the market for AI technologies.
