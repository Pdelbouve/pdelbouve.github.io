## Working Papers

### Diffusion de l'intelligence artificielle en France
*with Philippe Aghion, Antonin Bergeaud and Simon Bunel*  [[Paper](/files/aghion2025diffusion.pdf)]

<button class="abstract-toggle-button" type="button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="abstract-1">Show abstract</button>
<div class="paper-abstract-container" id="abstract-1" hidden>
  <p class="paper-abstract">
Using more than 120 million online job postings in France since 2019, 
we document the diffusion of artificial intelligence across firms, sectors 
and regions, and study its effects on labor demand. We find that AI adoption 
is highly concentrated and is associated with higher productivity, revenues 
and employment, together with a reallocation of hiring across occupations.
  </p>
</div>

### Innovation and Competition in the AI Industry: Is Frontier AI Development Sustainable?
[[Paper](/files/Innovation_and_Competition_in_the_AI_Industry.pdf)] · [[Policy note](/files/LIFT_PolicyPaper_2026-04_English.pdf)]

<button class="abstract-toggle-button" type="button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="abstract-2">Show abstract</button>
<div class="paper-abstract-container" id="abstract-2" hidden>
  <p class="paper-abstract">
Developing frontier AI is increasingly costly. Another feature of the AI industry, however, is the speed at which competitors catch up technologically, exposing frontier developers to intense price competition. Can frontier AI development remain economically viable under these conditions? With OpenRouter usage data we study competition and innovation dynamics in the AI sector. Event-study estimates associate frontier-model releases with a 69% increase in providers’ revenue and lower-cost catch-up with a 26% decline in exposed incumbents’ revenue. Users nevertheless redirect spending toward new, higher-quality models from incumbent providers, while frontier users continue to favor high-quality alternatives. These patterns are consistent with defensive innovation and persistent demand for frontier capabilities. We develop and calibrate a Schumpeterian-style model reflecting AI’s general-purpose character: frontier advances open additional uses, while cheaper catch-up broadens adoption of established capabilities. Our preliminary quantitative analysis shows continued frontier progress even under catch-up and partial exhaustion of new uses, with investment particularly sensitive to background demand growth. Alternative scenarios illustrate how stronger protection of innovation rewards can support frontier research and further automation while raising the cost of accessing established capabilities. These findings link frontier sustainability to market expansion and the balance between innovation rewards and affordable diffusion.
  </p>
</div>

### Consistent metropolitan boundaries for the remote sensing of urban land

*with Michiel N. Daams, Alexandre Banquet and Paolo Veneri*  [[Paper](/files/daams2023consistent.pdf)] *[Remote Sensing of Environment, 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425723003401)*

<button class="abstract-toggle-button" type="button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="abstract-3">Show abstract</button>
<div class="paper-abstract-container" id="abstract-3" hidden>
  <p class="paper-abstract">
We introduce an internationally consistent definition of metropolitan areas 
for remote-sensing applications and apply it to 687 European metropolitan 
areas. Combining Sentinel satellite imagery with deep learning, we show that 
the choice of urban boundaries matters for both estimated land use and 
classification accuracy.
  </p>
</div>



### Monitoring land use in cities using satellite imagery and deep learning

*with Alexandre Banquet, Michiel N. Daams and Paolo Veneri*  [[Paper](/files/veneri2022monitoring.pdf)] *[OECD Regional Development Papers, 2022](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/monitoring-land-use-in-cities-using-satellite-imagery-and-deep-learning_dc8e85d5-en.html)*

<button class="abstract-toggle-button" type="button" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="abstract-4">Show abstract</button>
<div class="paper-abstract-container" id="abstract-4" hidden>
  <p class="paper-abstract">
We develop a deep-learning approach to monitor urban land use from Sentinel 
satellite imagery. Applying a U-Net model to 687 European metropolitan areas, 
we map residential and business-related land use and provide a scalable 
framework for tracking urban development consistently and in near real time.
  </p>
</div>
